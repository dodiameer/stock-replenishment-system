COMPANY INVENTORY POLICY 2026

RULE 1 - PERISHABLE GOODS: 
If an item is a food product or perishable (e.g., Sushi Rice, Produce, Dairy),
you must NEVER order more than 40% of the maximum warehouse capacity, regardless of what the demand forecast says. 

RULE 2 - FINANCIAL CAP: 
No single automated reorder can exceed $1,500 in total cost.
If the calculated order exceeds this amount, you must reduce the order quantity until the total cost is strictly under $1,500.

RULE 3 - SUPPLIER MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity):
Orders placed with the supplier "Fivebridge" must have a minimum order quantity of 
at least 100 units to qualify for free freight.
If the suggested order for this supplier is below 100, you must increase it to exactly 100.
