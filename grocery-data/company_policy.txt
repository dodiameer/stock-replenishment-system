COMPANY INVENTORY POLICY 2026

RULE 1 - PERISHABLE GOODS: 
If an item is a perishable good of one of these categories (Fruits & Vegetables, Dairy, Seafood, Bakery),
you must NEVER order more than 40% of the maximum warehouse capacity, regardless of what the demand forecast says. 
Only rely on the category specified in the provided information, do not attempt to classify the category yourself. 

RULE 2 - FINANCIAL CAP: 
No single automated reorder can exceed $250 per a single item.
If the calculated item order exceeds this amount, you must reduce the order quantity until the total cost is strictly under $250.
Additionally, no cart total can exceed $1500. If the calculated cart total exceeds this amount, you must reduce the quantity as equally from each item as possible until the cart total is under $1500

RULE 3 - SUPPLIER MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity):
Orders placed with the supplier "Fivebridge" must have a minimum order quantity of 
at least 100 units to qualify for free freight.
If the suggested order for this supplier is below 100, you must increase it to exactly 100 unless it breaches rule 1 or exceeds the maximum warehouse capacity for that item.
